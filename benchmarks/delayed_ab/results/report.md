Cause: the refactor removed most of the linear per-node cost of building a chain and could not touch the quadratic one, which lives in a frozen module: HighLevelGraph.__init__ re-wraps every layer through an isinstance pass on construction, which is O(layers) per node and therefore O(n^2) over a chain.
